# Literature Review: Text -> Big Five, World-Class Scores & Best Practices

Evidence base for the modeling choices in `SOLUTION.md`, with emphasis on
2024-2026 work. All correlations are Pearson r unless noted.

**Ceiling caveat:** every published raw r > 0.40 uses richer inputs (long
dialogues, autobiographies, huge corpora) or fine-tuning. For <=5 short answers
there is no published raw r > 0.40. Our 0.3215 is at/above the short-text frontier.

## Answers to four design questions

1. **Item batteries for short-text persona simulation.** Field uses BFI/BFI-2,
   IPIP-NEO, HEXACO, TIPI, mini-IPIP with reverse-scored per-trait means. No clean
   head-to-head on short elicited text (a literature gap). Liu et al. argue for
   facet-balanced batteries (BFI-2's 15 facets) since finer items give more
   distinct behavioral anchors. Our 30-item balanced BFI-2-style battery is
   defensible but under-benchmarked.
2. **Persona vs observer framing.** First-person persona wins but the gap is
   modest (Yang et al.: client .426 > observer .386). LLM inference is on par with
   human observers (~.27). We use persona as default; an observer variant is cheap
   ensemble diversity.
3. **Fine-tuning small models on n~1000.** Feasible (Gemma-2-2b, LoRA Llama-3.1-8B
   per trait) but risky: the big counselling gains came from rich dialogues, and
   the 2026 Evaluation Drift result shows variance drops while joint-profile
   accuracy can stay near chance. Not pursued here.
4. **Raw r > 0.40 on short text.** No precedent. Confirms our ceiling framing.

## Mechanism (Liu et al. 2025)

LLMs role-playing a person run a two-stage process: (1) compress raw responses
into a natural-language persona summary ("sufficient statistic"); (2) answer items
from that summary. Adding the summary back as input *improves* accuracy
("SummaryAdded"). Robust to item reordering (k ~= 1.42 across order conditions).

We implemented and piloted this two-stage path; on our short SJIs it was a wash on
mean r (0.273 vs 0.279) and was not shipped (see `NEGATIVE_RESULTS.md`).

## Extracted prompt templates (for reference / future tuning)

### Liu et al. 2025 -- ScoreOnly persona prompt (paraphrased)
```
SYSTEM: Please imagine you are role-playing a specific person. The following are
descriptions of your personality, each with a level of applicability
(Strongly Disagree ... Strongly Agree):
[20 Big Five items with the person's response]
Please rate how much the person you are role-playing would agree with the
following descriptions. [Likert legend] Output 'Description 1:(int);Description 2:(int)...'
USER: [target scale items]
```

### Liu et al. 2025 -- SummaryAdded (the key lever)
Identical to ScoreOnly, plus:
```
The following is a supplementary summary generated by a model, which you may
refer to as you see fit:
Key Points Summary: [LLM-generated persona summary]
```

### Zhu et al. 2025 -- BFI-10 item-level observer prompt (Table 5)
```
You are a clinical psychologist specializing in personality assessment. Based on
the individual's response below, infer how they might score on each BFI-10 item.
Participant Response: {response}
Assign scores only based on what is *actually expressed*, using 1-5.
Output: BFI_1_score: X ... BFI_10_score: X
```

### Zhu et al. 2025 -- CoT trait prompt (Table 7)
```
You are a psychologically insightful agent... reflect on the speaker's language,
behavior, and implicit attitudes signaling Big Five traits.
Step-by-step: 1) summarize tone/themes; 2) identify cues per trait;
3) justify scores in 2-3 sentences. Score each trait 1.0-5.0 (half points allowed).
```
